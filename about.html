About Nathan

Profile

I am a Flight Operations and Dispatch trainee with a solid foundation in flight planning, meteorology interpretation, and aviation regulation. My work is built on precision: reading a METAR correctly, tracking a NOTAM that actually affects a flight, and knowing when a number needs a second check rather than a guess. Alongside this technical base, I have spent the past months building fluency in applying AI tools to real dispatch tasks — not as a novelty, but as a way to compress hours of manual triage into a process a human can still fully verify and stand behind.
