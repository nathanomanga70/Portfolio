Since July 2026, I have worked as a General AI Fluency Intern at FlyRank, applying AI tools to real aviation dispatch tasks under a structured weekly assignment series. The work is deliberately hands-on: each assignment produces a working artifact, not a slide about AI.

Sub-project

AI-Powered NOTAM / METAR / TAF Briefing Assistant

The internship's capstone project: an AI assistant (built on Claude) that takes raw NOTAM and METAR/TAF text and produces a structured, severity-tiered operational brief — decoding ICAO abbreviations into plain language and classifying each item as Critical, Moderate, or Informational. The system prompt enforces one non-negotiable rule, drawn directly from aviation safety culture: flag uncertainty rather than guess.
